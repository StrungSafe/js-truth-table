Truth Table Generator

Type a boolean expression and get its truth table. Variables are detected automatically.

Operators

Type any of these into your expression — words and symbols both work.

Meaning Word Symbol Also accepts Example
Negation (not) NOT ¬ ! NOT p
Conjunction (and) AND ∧ && p AND q
Disjunction (or) OR ∨ || p OR q
Exclusive or XOR ⊕ ^ p XOR q
Implication IMPLIES → -> p IMPLIES q
Biconditional IFF ↔ <-> p IFF q
Constants TRUE / FALSE — — p AND TRUE

Use parentheses to group, e.g. p AND (q OR NOT r). Variable names can be any letters/digits (e.g. p, q, isValid).
